# M6: Sequencing ("the brain") — in progress

Predict the next pitch type from game context, as a probability distribution to **sample** from.
Pitchers: Ohtani (660271, 2025–26), Misiorowski (694819, 2025–26, his 2025 "SL" relabelled FC),
Sale (519242, 2026). Real Statcast data only.

Reproduce: `PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/m6_evaluate.py` (writes `results/m6_folds.csv`,
`m6_reliability.csv`, `m6_calibration.png`).

## How it's measured

- **Split by game, 5-fold.** Every game is held out exactly once, so no at-bat or game leaks
  between training and testing. Numbers are pitch-weighted means over the 5 folds.
- **Metric:** held-out log loss (mean −log p of the pitch actually thrown; lower is better), plus
  pooled one-vs-rest calibration (reliability bins).
- **Hyperparameters** (smoothing `a`, logistic `C`, boosting rounds) are picked per fold on a 30%
  game slice of that fold's *training* games (nested CV), never on test games.
- Seed 0 throughout.

## Models

| model | features | file |
|---|---|---|
| mix | none (pitcher's overall pitch mix) | reference |
| count | balls-strikes, Dirichlet-smoothed toward the mix | `sequence/baseline.py` |
| logistic | count, batter side, previous pitch in AB (one-hot, multinomial, L2) | `sequence/logistic.py` |
| logistic+tto | logistic + times through the order | same |
| boosted | gradient-boosted trees on the logistic+tto features | same |

## Results (held-out log loss)

| pitcher | pitches (games) | mix | count | logistic | logistic+tto | boosted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ohtani | 2088 (28) | 1.553 | 1.509 | 1.425 | **1.418** | 1.433 |
| Misiorowski | 3811 (45) | 1.159 | 1.121 | 1.071 | 1.066 | **1.064** |
| Sale | 2574 (27) | 1.178 | 1.133 | 1.002 | 0.996 | **0.990** |

- **Every model beats the count baseline on held-out games** (logistic: 5/5 folds for all three;
  gain 0.050–0.131, fold SD 0.02–0.04).
- **Boosted trees ≈ logistic.** Fold wins vs logistic+tto: Ohtani 1/5, Misiorowski 2/5, Sale 4/5.
  The tuner picked the fewest rounds on the grid for Ohtani (25), i.e. it prefers a simpler model:
  with 2–4k pitches, interactions don't pay for their variance. **logistic+tto is the default.**

### What each feature is worth

Log loss added by *removing* a feature from logistic:

| pitcher | count | batter side | previous pitch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ohtani | 0.046 | 0.055 | 0.006 |
| Misiorowski | 0.036 | 0.018 | 0.025 |
| Sale | 0.043 | 0.097 | 0.006 |

Log loss saved by *adding* one feature to logistic (folds improved):

| pitcher | times through order | pitch of game | outs | runners | score diff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ohtani | +0.008 (3/5) | +0.004 (3/5) | 0.000 | −0.003 | −0.013 |
| Misiorowski | +0.005 (5/5) | +0.005 (5/5) | +0.001 | +0.003 | −0.007 |
| Sale | +0.007 (4/5) | +0.006 (5/5) | −0.003 | −0.003 | −0.002 |

Batter side dominates for Sale; previous pitch only matters for Misiorowski. Times through the
order is small but consistent; pitch of game carries the same (fatigue) signal. Outs, runners and
score are noise at this sample size, and adding all five at once overfits.

## Calibration

Pooled over all held-out pitches (`m6_calibration.png`). Largest |observed − predicted| in any
bin with ≥100 forecasts: logistic 0.022–0.028, except Ohtani 0.071 (top bin: predicted 0.64,
observed 0.57, n = 166, slightly overconfident). Boosted 0.027–0.043. The count table's apparent
underconfidence at high p in the first single-split run was small-bin noise (n = 3–26).
