# M6: Sequencing ("the brain") — in progress

Predict the next pitch type from game context, as a probability distribution to **sample** from.
Pitchers: Ohtani (660271, 2025–26), Misiorowski (694819, 2025–26, his 2025 "SL" relabelled FC),
Sale (519242, 2026). Real Statcast data only.

Reproduce: `PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/m6_evaluate.py` (writes `results/m6_folds.csv`,
`m6_reliability.csv`, `m6_calibration.png`) and `scripts/m6_transformer.py` (needs the `seq`
extra, i.e. torch; writes `m6_transformer_*`).

## How it's measured

- **Split by game, 5-fold.** Every game is held out exactly once, so no at-bat or game leaks
  between training and testing. Numbers are pitch-weighted means over the 5 folds.
- **Metric:** held-out log loss (mean −log p of the pitch actually thrown; lower is better), plus
  pooled one-vs-rest calibration (reliability bins).
- **Hyperparameters** (smoothing `a`, logistic `C`, boosting rounds) are picked per fold on a 30%
  game slice of that fold's *training* games (nested CV), never on test games.
- Seed 0 throughout.

## Models

| model | features | file |
|---|---|---|
| mix | none (pitcher's overall pitch mix) | reference |
| count | balls-strikes, Dirichlet-smoothed toward the mix | `sequence/baseline.py` |
| logistic | count, batter side, previous pitch in AB (one-hot, multinomial, L2) | `sequence/logistic.py` |
| logistic+tto | logistic + times through the order | same |
| boosted | gradient-boosted trees on the logistic+tto features | same |

## Calibration

Pooled over all held-out pitches (`m6_calibration.png`). Largest |observed − predicted| in any
bin with ≥100 forecasts: logistic 0.022–0.028, except Ohtani 0.071 (top bin: predicted 0.64,
observed 0.57, n = 166, slightly overconfident). Boosted 0.027–0.043. The count table's apparent
underconfidence at high p in the first single-split run was small-bin noise (n = 3–26).

## Transformer (`sequence/transformer.py`, `scripts/m6_transformer.py`)

A 2-layer causal transformer over the pitches of each at-bat: pitch t attends to every earlier
pitch's type, result (ball / called / whiff / foul) and zone, plus count, side, times through
order. **Pooled** over the three pitchers with a pitcher embedding and per-pitcher arsenal mask
(one pitcher alone is too little data). Same 5 game folds. Width / dropout / weight decay and
epoch count are chosen per fold on held-out *training* games; every fold chose (32, 0.3, 0.1),
15–22 epochs. A second logistic reference gets the same new inputs (previous result and zone), so
a transformer gain over it would have to come from the longer history or the pooling.

| pitcher | logistic+tto | logistic+prev result/zone | transformer | folds beating logistic+tto |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ohtani | 1.418 | **1.413** | 1.421 | 2/5 |
| Misiorowski | 1.066 | **1.065** | 1.070 | 2/5 |
| Sale | **0.996** | 1.002 | 1.003 | 1/5 |

- **The transformer does not beat the logistic model** (0.004–0.007 worse). The previous pitch's
  result and zone don't help the logistic either (±0.006, mixed sign).
- **It is overconfident for the two smaller pitchers:** largest bin gap 0.19 (Ohtani) and 0.12
  (Sale) vs 0.04 for Misiorowski and ≤0.06 for the logistic models. Its first, lightly
  regularised config was worse still (Ohtani 1.437, bin gap 0.21). Pooled early stopping is
  dominated by Misiorowski's larger sample, so the smaller pitchers get overfit.
- Reading: with 2–4k pitches per pitcher, pitch history beyond the count and the last pitch
  carries little that generalises. Untried: per-pitcher temperature scaling (Guo et al. 2017),
  pooling all 8 cached pitchers, history across at-bats in a game.

## Still wrong / not done

- Per-pitcher data is small (27–45 games); gains under ~0.01 are near the noise floor.
- **Default model: logistic+tto.** It wins or ties everywhere and is the best calibrated.
- Target location is done (next section); a 2-component mixture for it is not tried.

## Target location (`sequence/location.py`, `scripts/m6_location.py`)

Where the pitch crosses the plate, as a distribution to **sample** (`LocationModel.sample`).
Reproduce: `PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/m6_location.py` (writes `results/m6_location_folds.csv`,
`m6_location_types.csv`). Real Statcast data only; the unit test uses labelled synthetic data.

- **Target:** (x, z) in feet where Statcast's own 9-parameter fit crosses the **front** of the plate
  (y = 17/12 ft, `statcast.fit_plate_crossing`), not `plate_x/plate_z` (mid-plate in the 2026 CSVs).
  No row lacked the fit columns, so none dropped for missing location.
- **Reference:** per pitcher and pitch type, one 2D Gaussian on the training games.
- **Conditioned:** per pitch type, same Gaussian but the mean shifts with count, batter side and
  previous pitch (one-hot, ridge); 2x2 residual covariance shared across contexts (mean shift only).
  Ridge alpha picked per fold on a 30% game slice of the training games (grid 1-300).
- **Eval:** same 5 game folds; mean log-likelihood of the true (x, z) per pitch (nats per ft^2,
  higher is better), pitch-weighted. Pitch types with <30 training pitches in a fold are skipped:
  only 3 Misiorowski pitches (of 3811) dropped, 0 for the others.

| pitcher | pitches | reference | conditioned | gain | fold SD of gain | folds beating reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ohtani | 2088 | -2.311 | -2.262 | +0.049 | 0.025 | 5/5 |
| Misiorowski | 3808 | -2.211 | -2.187 | +0.024 | 0.009 | 5/5 |
| Sale | 2574 | -2.080 | -1.987 | +0.094 | 0.018 | 5/5 |
| Cease | 6064 | -2.396 | -2.273 | +0.123 | 0.007 | 5/5 |
| Yamamoto | 5522 | -2.117 | -2.032 | +0.085 | 0.017 | 5/5 |
| King | 2981 | -2.266 | -2.164 | +0.102 | 0.019 | 5/5 |
| Sanchez | 3158 | -2.150 | -2.086 | +0.065 | 0.010 | 5/5 |
| Schlittler | 4244 | -2.288 | -2.234 | +0.053 | 0.009 | 5/5 |

- **Calibration** (held-out pitches inside the central ellipses, pooled): 50% ellipse 0.482 / 0.496 /
  0.480, 90% ellipse 0.886 / 0.895 / 0.893 (Ohtani / Misiorowski / Sale). Slightly under-covered at
  50% (in-sample residuals understate the spread a little). Per pitch type (n 59-2319) it ranges 0.43-0.53
  and 0.81-0.90, mostly small-sample noise; the worst are Ohtani FC (n=59) and FS.
- **Median miss of the model mean:** 8-14 in, by type (`m6_location_types.csv`); fastballs ~9-10 in,
  curveballs and splitters/changeups 12-14 in. That is mostly the pitch-to-pitch scatter, not model error.

### What's still wrong

- Gains are small in absolute terms (0.02-0.09 nats) and the conditioned mean moves only a little;
  most of the location variance is unexplained noise. Sale's larger gain is probably batter side (a
  platoon aim shift), not shown separately.
- Shared covariance: scatter surely varies with count (e.g. wider when ahead), not modelled.
- Gaussian only: locations are bounded by what a pitcher can reasonably throw and are skewed
  (misses off the plate, down in the dirt). The 2-component mixture was **not tried**.
- No ablation of which feature drives the gain, and no fatigue / times-through-order feature.
- Location is not conditioned on the sampled pitch's flight (M2 release, spin): x/z here is independent
  of the physical release draw, so a full simulated pitch would need to reconcile the two.

## Addendum, 2026-11-20: all eight viewer pitchers

For the M7 viewer the evaluation and location scripts were extended from three pitchers to eight:
added Cease (656302, 2024 and 2026 CSVs), Yamamoto (808967, 2025-26), King (650633, 2026),
Sanchez (650911, 2026) and Schlittler (693645, 2025-26). The five new rows are in the tables above
(same 5 game folds, seed 0, nested hyperparameter choice). Rerun from scratch, the original three
rows reproduced exactly. Numbers are from `results/m6_folds.csv`, `m6_reliability.csv` and
`m6_location_folds.csv`; `m6_held_out.json` (tracked, read by the MCP server) was regenerated.

- **"Every model beats the count baseline on held-out games" holds for all eight.** Logistic,
  logistic+tto and boosted each beat the count baseline in 5/5 folds for every pitcher. Logistic
  gain over count: 0.038 (King) to 0.131 (Sale); the new pitchers 0.038-0.064.
- **Boosted vs logistic+tto flips for the new pitchers:** boosted wins 4-5 of 5 folds for all five
  (gain 0.006-0.017) where it lost for Ohtani. They have 3-6k pitches (Ohtani 2.1k). Gains are
  small; logistic+tto stays the simple default, boosted has the best raw loss.
- **Calibration** (largest bin gap, bins with >=100 forecasts): logistic 0.046 Cease, 0.045
  Yamamoto, 0.028 King, 0.034 Sanchez, 0.067 Schlittler; boosted 0.022, 0.009, 0.031, 0.061, 0.029.
  Schlittler's logistic and Sanchez's boosted are the loosest of the five.
- **Feature worth:** batter side is the biggest for every new pitcher (Yamamoto's count is close:
  0.047 vs 0.049). Previous pitch is worth about 0.01 or less (Sanchez -0.001). Times-through-order and
  pitch-of-game gains are at most +0.004; outs, runners, score are noise or negative.
- **Location:** conditioned beats reference in 5/5 folds for all five (gain +0.053 to +0.123).
  Pooled coverage of the 50% / 90% ellipses: Cease 0.494 / 0.892, Yamamoto 0.489 / 0.887, King
  0.488 / 0.884, Sanchez 0.498 / 0.885, Schlittler 0.504 / 0.886. Dropped for <30 training
  pitches of a type: 19 Cease, 1 Yamamoto, 50 Schlittler; none for King and Sanchez; no rows
  lacked a location.
- **Transformer covers the original three only** (Ohtani, Misiorowski, Sale); it was not rerun
  (needs torch, not used by M7). `m6_held_out.json` has `"transformer": null` for the other five.
