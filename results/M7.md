# M7: "Face the pitcher" — simulated at-bats vs real held-out at-bats (DRAFT, Task 7)

Question: do at-bats played by the browser engine (`web/src/face/engine.js`, Average hitter) look
like the pitcher's real at-bats in games the models never saw? This is a **measurement, not a
gate**: nothing was tuned to pass.

Reproduce: `python scripts/m7_real_stats.py --all` then `node scripts/m7_check.mjs --all`
(outputs `results/m7/<slug>-real.json`, `<slug>-sim.json`, `checks.csv`, all local/gitignored).
Test: `tests/test_m7_real_stats.py` (hand-counted synthetic frame).

## What was measured

- **Real:** `split_by_game(seed=0)` TEST games of all seasons pooled (the pooled frame the M6 seq
  model is fitted on in `m7_export.py`; the bank/M2 arm uses the newest season only). Seasons per
  pitcher are in the table below. Only 8-18 held-out games each, so ~170-430 at-bats and wide intervals.
- **Real at-bat** = reaches a terminal event (strikeout, walk, ball in play). Excluded and counted:
  HBP, at-bats truncated by a non-pitch event (caught stealing etc.), intentional walks, at-bats
  with missing pitch data (no location/type/outcome). Stand = the batter's stand in that at-bat.
- **Sim:** 20,000 Average-hitter at-bats per pitcher, started at 0-0, stand drawn from the real
  `stand_share`, seed 0, times-through-order fixed at 1 (real includes all).
- **Statistics (per stand):** `zone_rate` = share of pitches with the engine's `isStrike` rule
  (zone medians from the face JSON; real uses the same heart/edge_in bins of `hitter.zone_bin` on
  the front-of-plate crossing); `first_pitch_strike` = share of at-bats whose 0-0 pitch is a called,
  swinging or foul strike, or in play (**definitional difference:** for a take, real uses the umpire's
  call, `called_strike`/`automatic_strike`, while the sim uses the zone rule `isStrike` on the true
  location; the same split applies to `zone_rate`, which is zone-rule in both but is not the umpire's
  zone); `pitches_per_ab`; `outcomes` (strikeout / walk / in_play
  shares of at-bats); `mix_by_count`.
- **Intervals:** 95% percentile bootstrap over **games**, 2,000 resamples, seed 0. A statistic is
  checked as inside/outside that interval (sim is not given an interval; at N=20,000 its own noise is small).
- **mix_by_count:** total-variation distance (TV) between sim and real mix per count vs the 95th
  percentile of the real bootstrap's TV from its own point mix (✓ if sim TV is below it). In `checks.csv` mix rows (`mix_tv@<count>`), `real` and `hi` hold that
  TV95 threshold and `lo` is 0; `sim` is the sim's TV from the real point mix. The sim's own sampling
  noise is not included in that comparison, which is fine at 20,000 at-bats. Counts
  with fewer than 20 real pitches are skipped. The real mix is **renormalised over the pitcher's
  bank types**, because excluded types are absent from the sim.
- **Batter-none mode** (every pitch a take) has no real counterpart; it is not graded.

## What's still wrong

- **Misses are real, not tuned away.** Ohtani is the worst (17/25): vs lefties the sim has too
  many strikeouts (0.318 vs 0.157-0.281), zone rate too high (0.536 vs 0.478-0.518), first-pitch
  strikes too high (0.626 vs 0.407-0.595) and in-play share too low. Sale vs lefties walks 0.070
  vs 0.000-0.059 (51 real ABs). Misiorowski vs lefties: too many strikeouts, too few balls in play.
- **first_pitch_strike falls below the interval for Cease L, King L and Schlittler R**:
  the sim under-throws first-pitch strikes there. Part of this may be definitional (umpire call
  for real takes vs zone rule for sim takes, see above). Other candidate causes (untested): the location model's 0-0
  context effect, the hitter model's 0-0 take/swing split, and the zone rule (medians vs per-pitch zone).
- **Small real samples.** 8-18 held-out games per pitcher; intervals are wide, so "inside" is a
  weak test. Failing a wide interval is more informative than passing it.
- **Fixed context in the sim:** tto = 1, Average hitter only; real at-bats mix all times through the
  order, all batters, runners and score. Real pooled seasons vs a bank built from the newest season
  only (Cease: 2024-26 data, 2026 arm).
- **Excluded types** (FC, CH, SI, SL, ST, PO per the table) cannot be thrown by the sim. Mix
  checks are renormalised without them; their real share is at most 5.2% (Ohtani).
- **mix TV is a coarse test:** with few real pitches in a count the bootstrap threshold is small
  and noisy (Sanchez R 1-0 and 1-2 fail on ~100 pitches), and counts with <20 pitches are skipped.
- **Truncated/HBP exclusions** drop 2-6 HBP at-bats per pitcher, which slightly inflates real
  strikeout/walk/in-play shares relative to a sim with no HBP.
